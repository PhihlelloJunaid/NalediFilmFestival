Naledi Film Festival
08-09 August 2025
VRYBURG, North West Province

About The Festival

A new star is rising in South Africa's creative landscape! The inaugural Naledi Film Festival will light up Vryburg from 8–9 August 2025, offering a powerful platform for young voices, bold stories, and authentic African narratives.

Organized by Rapitsi Development Projects in partnership with Monate Africa and the National Film and Video Foundation (NFVF), this youth-driven initiative is designed to empower aspiring filmmakers aged 16–35 from the Northwest Province with extended invitation to young filmmakers in Northern Cape and Free State provinces.

"Naledi" means star – a fitting name for a festival that aims to spotlight the next generation of creatives in Northwest Province. The festival serves not just as a celebration, but as an incubator for future talent, particularly in underserved and rural communities often left out of mainstream creative industry opportunities.

Admission to all festival events is FREE, with masterclass slots available on a first come, first served basis. The festival encourages participation from young people passionate about acting, filmmaking, storytelling, and cultural expression.
